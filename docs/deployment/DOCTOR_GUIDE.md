# TeleMed AI — Physician & Consultation Guide

This guide details how verified medical practitioners review AI-generated risk assessments and conduct virtual consultations.

---

## 1. Credential Verification
Before accessing patient records, physicians must submit state medical license credentials for administrator verification.

---

## 2. Patient Review Workspace
- **Pending Review Queue**: Filter patients by urgency, disease risk level, and assessment date.
- **AI Driver Preview**: Inspect top TreeSHAP biomarker drivers before entering the live session.

---

## 3. Consultation Workspace
- **Left Panel**: Review patient chronological timeline, past lab reports, and vitals.
- **Center Panel**: Author diagnostic notes, treatment plans, prescriptions, and follow-up dates.
- **Right Panel**: Interact with AI Clinical Assistant for evidence summaries and suggested questions.
