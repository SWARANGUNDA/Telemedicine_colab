# World Health Organization (WHO) Guidelines for Obesity Prevention and Management
Organization: World Health Organization (WHO)
Publication Date: 2023-06-15
Document Version: 2023.2
Evidence Type: CLINICAL_GUIDELINE

## Section 1: Classification and Adiposity Risk
Obesity is classified as Body Mass Index (BMI) >= 30.0 kg/m2. Central adiposity, indicated by waist circumference >= 102 cm in men and >= 88 cm in women, significantly increases cardiovascular and metabolic risk independently of BMI.

## Section 2: Multimodal Physical Activity and Telemetry Guidelines
Adults should engage in 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity physical activity per week. Wearable step telemetry indicates that increasing step count from 3,500 steps/day to 8,000–10,000 steps/day markedly reduces mortality and metabolic disease risk.

## Section 3: Sedentary Behavior and Sleep Duration
Reducing sedentary screen time (< 480 minutes/day) and maintaining optimal sleep duration (7 to 9 hours per night) are essential. Chronic short sleep (< 6 hours) impairs ghrelin/leptin regulation, increasing appetite and visceral fat deposition.
