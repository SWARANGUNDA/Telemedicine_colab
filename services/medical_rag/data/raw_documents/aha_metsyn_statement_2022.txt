# AHA/NHLBI Diagnosis and Management of Metabolic Syndrome
Organization: American Heart Association / NHLBI
Publication Date: 2022-09-10
Document Version: 2022.1
Evidence Type: CLINICAL_GUIDELINE

## Section 1: ATP III Harmonized Diagnostic Criteria
Metabolic Syndrome is diagnosed when 3 or more of the following 5 criteria are present:
1. Elevated waist circumference (>= 102 cm men, >= 88 cm women)
2. Elevated triglycerides (>= 150 mg/dL)
3. Reduced HDL cholesterol (< 40 mg/dL men, < 50 mg/dL women)
4. Elevated blood pressure (Systolic >= 130 mmHg or Diastolic >= 85 mmHg)
5. Elevated fasting glucose (>= 100 mg/dL)

## Section 2: Clinical Management Strategy
First-line therapy focuses on lifestyle changes targeting atherogenic dyslipidemia, hypertension, and insulin resistance. Reducing saturated fats (< 7% of daily calories) and increasing physical activity reduces inflammatory markers (CRP, ALT).
